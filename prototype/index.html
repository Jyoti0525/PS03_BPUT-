What Jeevia Does

Everything a Health Worker Needs,
Nothing They Don't

Designed for the realities of Indian healthcare — variable connectivity, multilingual patients, high patient load, and minimal specialist availability.

Multimodal Symptom Intake

Patients can speak, type, or dictate symptoms in their native language. Works in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, and 18+ other languages via Bhashini & IndicConformer.

OCR Lab Report Analysis

Upload handwritten or printed lab reports. PaddleOCR extracts values, flags abnormals, and structures findings into the triage note automatically.

AI Triage Summarization

Lightweight LLM (BioMistral-7B) generates a structured narrative summary — never a diagnosis — to support the reviewing clinician's decision.

22-Language Support

IndicTrans2 translation ensures every patient can communicate in their native tongue, and every clinician sees notes in their preferred language.

Human-Review Workflow

Every AI output goes to a qualified reviewer. Red/Yellow/Green triage queue keeps critical patients at the front. Zero autonomous decisions.

Privacy-First Design

Consent-gated intake, anonymized IDs, minimal data retention, immutable audit logs, and role-based access controls — compliant with DPDP Act 2023.

The Process

From Symptom to
Structured Review

A clear, three-step pipeline designed to be completed in under 4 minutes, keeping clinicians in full control.

1

Patient Intake

Patient speaks or types symptoms in any language. Uploads lab reports or images. System collects timeline, identifies missing info, and asks follow-up questions.

2

AI Summarization

Rules engine tags urgency (Red/Yellow/Green) using AIIMS ATP thresholds. LLM writes a structured triage note. OCR extracts lab values. Everything is clearly advisory.

3

Doctor Review

Clinician sees prioritized queue, reads the note, reviews conflicts, approves or edits, then decides on referral or treatment. AI only supports — never decides.

Important Safety Disclaimer

Jeevia is an educational prototype for triage-support purposes only. It is explicitly non-diagnostic: it organizes patient-provided information, highlights urgency signals, and supports faster qualified review — but does not prescribe treatment or replace a qualified medical professional. All AI-generated content is advisory and must be reviewed by licensed healthcare staff before any clinical decision is made. This system uses synthetic or public sample data only; no real patient records are used. Outputs must never be acted upon without professional medical judgment.
